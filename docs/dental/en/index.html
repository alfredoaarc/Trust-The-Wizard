The questions everyone asks

Is the first visit really free?

Really. We examine you, explain what's going on and give you a fixed written quote. If you decide to do nothing, nothing happens and you pay nothing.

Will it hurt?

Usually not: with modern anaesthesia most treatments don't hurt. If something might be uncomfortable, we tell you before — not during. And if you want to stop at any point, raise your hand and we stop.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes: financing up to 24 months, interest-free. An implant, for example, comes to €37/month. We work it out for you at the first visit, with no commitment.

What if the final price changes?

The quote we give you after the diagnosis is fixed: that's the price, unless you decide to change the treatment. The "from" prices on this site are guides so you can compare without calling.

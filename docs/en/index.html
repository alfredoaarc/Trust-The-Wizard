Is your website working against you?

Most business websites quietly turn visitors away. Here's how.

🐞

Errors on show

Broken links, unfilled template text, pages that throw errors. Visitors see it and lose trust.

📧

A Gmail address

A Gmail or Hotmail address instead of your own business one instantly looks less serious.

Years out of date

An old year in the footer or a dead blog tells people the business is asleep.

📱

It breaks on phones

Most people find you on their phone. If they have to pinch and zoom, they leave.

🔎

Google can't find you

If you don't turn up in search, people find your competitor first.

🚪

It brings no enquiries

No clear next step, no contact button — so people look around and then go.
